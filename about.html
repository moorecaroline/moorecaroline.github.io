About

Hi, I'm Caroline.

I'm a product manager with an engineering background, originally from Santa Fe, New Mexico. I like working where people, products, and systems meet.

Today I'm a product manager at a healthcare technology company. I lead user research and help define product priorities. I manage our Jira boards and keep developers and operations aligned, so that the team builds the right thing and ships it well.

I studied engineering at Dartmouth, where I earned an A.B. in Engineering Sciences with a minor in Human-Centered Design and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering with a focus on product design. That training still shapes how I work. I start with the people who will use something, then work through the details that make it real, from CAD and prototypes to models and testing.
