## The problem

Kids outgrow bikes quickly. Many bikes end up as waste, some families can't afford new ones, and kids often ride bikes that are the wrong size, which is less safe.

## The pivot

We started by exploring a new bike design. As we looked at the problem through a design-for-environment lens, we saw that a better bike was not the biggest lever. The bigger opportunity was a system: a bike share run through the school system, so bikes move to the next child as kids grow. _Add what made you change direction._
